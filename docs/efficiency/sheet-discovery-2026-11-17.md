# Sheet Sync discovery and delivery costs, November 17, 2026

Returning only job IDs from candidate discovery reduced BSON returned for five local mixed jobs from 1,814 to 110 bytes. Mongo still returned all five complete post-claim documents (2,259 bytes) for delivery. The idle drain saved zero document bytes. The production sample below describes the deployed baseline; the new projection was verified on a local replica.

## Flow and consumers

1. **Durable intent.** The coordinator and outbox build a resource/entity coalescing key. Normal upserts refresh the requested operation on eligible active work, retain the higher priority and earlier due time, and use the caller's transaction session where supplied. Delete jobs carry tombstone target identities for records that can no longer be reloaded. A transaction rollback proof checks the domain record and intent together. A queue or cron wake drives the durable jobs.
2. **Discovery and claims.** After checking the Lead tab migration fence and acquiring the drain lease, the worker creates its run. One bounded find selects pending/retrying candidates due now with an absent or expired job lease. Priority descends and creation time ascends. Every candidate supplies only its ID to a separate atomic claim, which checks current status and lease and returns the current complete document after acquiring ownership. The local race includes a due retry with an expired lease, an equal-priority older candidate, a fourth eligible candidate beyond the limit, a future retry, a currently leased candidate, expired processing and terminal jobs. One candidate loses to a competitor after discovery. The remaining two selected jobs complete in order and the fourth stays pending. The existing sort gives no guarantee for equal priority *and* equal creation time.
3. **Planning and provider reads/writes.** Each logical job reloads its canonical entity and reruns membership rules. The Booking chain adds the linked Lead, and the Cancellation chain loads its Booking and linked Lead as well as the Cancellation. The mixed fixture issued three Form reads, three Booking reads, one Call read and one Cancellation read. The writer grouped matching physical writes across subscribers and used tab and Mongo row identity before changing membership. Its four physical appends covered Forms, Calls, Booked Deals and Cancelled Deals. Tab topology, reads, headers, formatting and clears account for the other 25 workbook requests. The mixed drain also issued 130 lease/fence reads. This projection changes only the payload of the single discovery find.
4. **Metadata and finalization.** Confirmed or uncertain outcomes merge by physical document before owned job finalization. On the mixed drain one update each went to Form (264 bytes), Call (264), Booking (273) and Cancellation (279). One bulk attempt insert held 16 logical outcomes. Five claim operations and five finalizing job updates returned through `findAndModify`. The run was inserted and finalized once. Physical work coalescing preserves independent retry hints, status and completion identity. Idle work still writes run history and maintains the lease. The earlier overlap comparison measured three metadata updates becoming one on both uncertainty and recovery. [Metadata comparison](sheet-metadata-2026-11-17.md)
5. **Operational and historical readers.** Admin health groups all jobs by status, reads the oldest open job and latest run. Job/run lists page sorted records plus a separate total; run detail reads all attempts for its run. Systems reads group job health and consult the latest run. Sheet Contains looks up active jobs by entity and chooses the first sorted match. Job Number Timeline reads job history for related Lead, Booking and Cancellation IDs, respecting the caller's optional evidence bound. None of these reads was included in drain command counts. Their existing contracts still consume the stored jobs, runs and attempts.

## Bounded production baseline and release interpretation

A verified read-only sample from the prior deployment found 16 idle, seven single-job and two multiple-job runs among the newest 25. Eleven jobs were claimed and synced across those runs. A separate newest-25 job sample contained 11,099 stored BSON bytes, or 550 bytes projected to IDs. Those samples include already-finalized jobs and cannot be paired to establish per-drain discovery savings, trigger mix across a day or physical overlap. [Target identity, operation bounds and aggregate evidence](../operations/evidence/sheet-production-baseline-2026-11-17.json)

The change adds `.select({ _id: 1 }).lean()` to the find in `claimDueJobs`. W6's additional candidate fields serve its separate reclaim/slot policy and have no consumer on this branch. The projection requires no flag, index or migration. Reverting those two chained calls restores the broad read. Comparable post-release samples of eligible work, completed/deferred/failed counts and downstream membership still require separate deployment and observation.
