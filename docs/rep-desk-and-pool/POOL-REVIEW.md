## Read-only production API scope

Canonical GET reads are within the Owner's explicit Phase 2 matching authority. Existing authenticated APIs, rather than a direct production database connection, supply the data. Form browse enrichment aggregates message-presence flags; it does not send SMS or write Leads. Minimize returned contact/money fields immediately in memory and persist only stable redacted identity keys and policy flags.

For complete best-effort identity traversal, page `limit=250` until `has_next_page=false`, recording each page's `total`, unique ids, repeated ids and scope. **Both FormLead and CallLead default to excluding `duplicate=true`; both need a separate duplicate pass.** Booked browse has no default source/leadless restriction when those parameters are omitted, so it includes attached, leadless/referral and pending intake anchors. Form `job_no` is accepted but ignored; no supported priority filter exists, and unknown query keys are stripped. Call/Booked Job matching is substring-based and needs existing exact-normalized/prefix-equivalent client checks. Avoid current-month filters on the canonical corpus: a current-month provider row can match an older Lead.

Single-field sort plus offset pagination offers no stable snapshot or guaranteed id tie. Constant totals alone do not rule out replacement/concurrent writes. Report traversal honestly and re-read changed/ambiguous candidates before a separately approved effect. Search, CSV export (5,000-row cap), first-linked-Lead Job timeline and single-Booking lifecycle projection do not establish exhaustive collision absence.

Run listing returns at most the latest 100 runs without history pagination. It cannot prove that no older applying run exists. The existing POST preview itself is guidance-only, but queue wake-up can process already-approved applying work. The coordinator therefore uses existing GET evidence and has not initiated a new preview, `/worker`, approval or apply call for this discovery.

The coordinator's [measured canonical crawl](evidence/phase2/canonical-priority9-collisions.json) (`2026-10-14T03:26:37Z`–`03:27:04Z`) contains 9,069 distinct Lead ids: Form ordinary/duplicate passes 7,057/166 and Call ordinary/duplicate passes 1,556/290. All four passes reached their observed totals without repeated ids. This establishes a useful canonical9 identity corpus, not actual current-month Granot Pool membership or transactional uniqueness. The [run/API evidence](evidence/phase2/read-api-surface.json) records eight applying runs among the returned latest-100 scope.

**Booking anchor coverage remains incomplete.** Full Booked browse failed with a legacy Boolean `true` cancellation reference being populated as a CancelledLead id. The `cancelled=false` browse query is a presence filter: it includes missing/null values and excludes both legacy false/true Booleans as well as cancellation references. That narrower pass reached 896 ids; six additional attached anchors raised the observed Booking count to 902. Unlinked cancelled/legacy-false/job-only anchors may still be absent, and population can hide dangling raw pointers. ## Model and feature boundary review

The proposed canonical Lead reuse plus explicit membership is coherent with the current identity constraints: multiple Leads may share a Job, and matching must compare both models, duplicates, equivalent identities, active links and official/pending/malformed Booking anchors. The proposals hold ambiguous, official, cancelled, bad and closed subjects; they do not mint a second Lead, merge aliases, overwrite another priority or reopen a closed period. Source/accepted-fact ordering and manual receiver protection remain authoritative.

The residual-pointer hold here is a **Pool identity/admission** rule, not a new financial officialness rule. Phase 1 prevents malformed residual pointers from falling back to official leadless credit; its canonical referral flag can still establish official referral allocation credit independently. Pool discovery holds incomplete identity even when a Booking is financially official. No prototype or read-report classification changes the authoritative money predicate.

Shared membership does not change `receiver_agent`. One logical daily slot is keyed by opportunity, New York working day and `pool_call`; policy versions/membership episodes are provenance rather than extra uniqueness dimensions. Reentry and multiple viewers cannot create another quota. Missed history is audit-only: it adds no Pool catch-up quota, accumulated call debt or per-viewer duty; a later applicable day opens only its own one slot. Provider-qualified event-time evidence and caller attribution remain separate from receiver and advisory claim. The calendar, late entry, same-day cross-mode coverage transfer, correction/waiver handling and inbound qualification are concrete Owner decisions. Codes 2/3 and their ordinary Daily catch-up rules remain unchanged, closed periods remain closed, and priority9 remains excluded from assigned potential. Pool sharing creates no money allocation for viewers.

The final nine decision rows are coherent with those fixed requirements. They distinguish no-create initial identity strategy and provider/canonical authority; shared membership permissions and advisory coordination; actual outbound/answered-inbound qualification without silently widening goal scopes; NY calendar/late entry/transfer; audit-only missed work; durable sheet origin and existing-match choice; measured collection horizon/budget; and a separately reviewed exact-id pilot/rollback. A general design approval cannot waive missing live evidence, incomplete Booking anchors or the later production manifest gate. No automatic admission of the 340 canonical9 records is recommended.

The dedicated authorization/DTO/BFF/live proposal preserves private assigned reads and adds membership-based entitlement only after approval. It requires server-wide filter/count/sort/cursor scope, detail revalidation, role denial, committed invalidation to all eligible Pool readers, exit eviction and authorized reconnect resnapshots. None of those capabilities is implemented by these documents.

Pool-created Lead exclusion must be immutable creation provenance enforced by central sheet planning and immediately before execution, including duplicate/bad routes, retries, repair and exit. Existing `no_sync` deliberately differs on duplicate/bad routes and is insufficient alone. Existing ordinary-synced matches retain history and ordinary behavior pending an exact Owner choice; no sheet deletion, column addition or ordinary-sync change is authorized. Rollback must retain the durable exclusion while Pool-origin Leads exist.
