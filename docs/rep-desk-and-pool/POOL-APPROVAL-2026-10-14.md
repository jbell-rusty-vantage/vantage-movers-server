# Priority Pool — approval-ready proposal

**October 14, 2026. Design and runnable local prototype only. Stop here for Owner approval before production feature implementation, cron/configuration changes or Pool-specific mutations.** This document supersedes the older Pool approval draft's access blockers, current-month collection recommendation and statements that priority9 has no meaning. [Live findings and provenance](POOL-FINDINGS-2026-10-14.md) supply the evidence; [prototype](../../ops/pool-prototype/README.md) supplies the reviewable behavior.

## Fixed requirements

Pool is Granot priority **9**. Retain ordinary Form Leads/Call Leads and their Source Company, Source Granularity, Ingestion Origin and `receiver_agent`. All trusted eligible Desk Reps, plus Owner and Managers, can see and work every eligible Pool Lead regardless of assignment. Show **New / Quoted / Other / Pool** as adjacent filters. There is **one shared daily call requirement per canonical Lead**, fulfilled by any eligible Rep. Completion clears it for everyone. Assigned Pool workload participates in the assigned Rep's workload design; verified calls are attributed to the actual caller. Ordinary private assigned queues, manual/source-less receiver protections, ordinary Sheet Sync and financial rules remain authoritative.

Evidence: 843 actual Pool rows in the declared 2020–today survey, 602 canonical valid existing matches, 241 held. 227 rows are older than 90 days; 823 are older than the current month. 92 canonical valid matches are unassigned. 72 committed transitions across 71 anchored Leads demonstrate that existing Leads really do change into Pool, while history for the remainder is unknown. Do not invent a Pool Lead model, duplicate existing Leads, auto-admit the entire inventory or “fix” the held rows as part of rollout.

## Smallest coherent model

Keep the canonical Lead as the opportunity. Extend server Outreach policy with a distinct **Pool shared workflow**, rather than mapping9 to ordinary `daily`: ordinary Daily reads and assignment-based requirements cannot express this entitlement. Add a small versioned shared-work record referencing canonical `{model,id}` and its accepted priority/eligibility boundary; it records activation/exit episodes and the shared calendar policy. This can extend the existing subject/period infrastructure; it must also support an eligible Lead without an existing ordinary subject. Do not introduce a second admission source of truth or a second Lead. The single canonical membership command/projector revalidates identity, source, accepted fact, closure and eligibility.

One duty key is **canonical Lead + New York date + `pool_call`**, enforced by a unique database index. Episode, receiver, viewer, claim, policy version and retry ID are provenance, never extra uniqueness dimensions. Entry/reentry or receiver changes cannot create another same-day quota. No retroactive debt is created by inspection, migration or admission. Missed dates remain team history; tomorrow has one new duty, no additional catch-up call.

Initial enrollment reuses only verified existing Leads with a coherent source/identity/Record Link/Booking result and no bad/duplicate/unmatched/cancelled/booked/closed hold. Unassigned eligible Leads remain visible with an Unassigned badge; they do not receive an invented individual workload owner. Current lack of an ordinary subject is not itself disqualification. Existing closed subjects require an explicit reviewed reopening decision; never reopen them merely because priority9 is visible. A reviewed pilot manifest must be freshly generated after approval; these captures are not such a manifest.

## Collection recommendation and time range

**Extend the existing application automation scheduler/queue with a Pool lane; do not create a Codex chat automation and do not change the existing ALL schedule/scope.** Reuse the corrected Estimates collector, source resolution, canonical planning, lifecycle capture/apply and `granot:automation:account` lease. Add an explicit validated `pool` versus `all` selector to the future run contract and deterministic identity; it cannot be an unvalidated arbitrary filter or an ALL run relabeled Pool. Keep both normalizers and all effect/closure gates. Pool ingestion here means reconcile existing Leads; no new Lead creation is proposed.

Recommended schedule, always resolved in America/New_York:

| Lane | Time | Open Date scope | Healthy freshness target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing ALL | 08:00, 13:00, 18:00 | Existing trailing90 inclusive dates plus older verified provider-date months | Unchanged |
| Pool refresh | 08:00, 13:00, 18:00, serialized after the corresponding ALL collection | Trailing90 inclusive dates **plus every older month ever observed for Pool**, unioned with existing provider-date evidence | Same working-day slots; five hours between daytime slots and 14 hours overnight |
| Pool historical discovery | 06:00 daily | Full declared survey floor **2020-01-01 through today**, partitioned and resumable; expand to the actual retained-history floor if Owner/provider evidence establishes an older floor | Within24h for previously unknown older entries in this declared range |

The dates above are **Open Dates, not modification dates**. A fixed 30/90/365-day rolling lookback cannot guarantee accuracy for an older Lead newly moved into Pool. Membership and remembered months never age out automatically. A nightly full-range sweep catches older newly-Pool rows when webhooks were missed; the foreground refresh avoids repeatedly scanning years of empty ranges at every slot. The actual earliest result is May23,2025, but choosing that as a permanent floor would silently miss later priority changes to still-older Leads. January2020 is the inspected floor, **not proof nothing exists before it**; approve or extend that boundary explicitly. Reconcile ALL across previously Pool months too, so positive non9 evidence can detect exits. An absent row in a Pool-selected report never proves exit, cancellation or deletion.

Use seven-day durable foreground partitions for compatibility with the existing ALL lane. The Pool-only historical sweep may begin with the **31-day partitions actually proven complete here**, splitting adaptively to seven-day/single-day as needed. 80 such historical partitions returned 843 rows with reconciled totals and no pagination. This survey cost 88 total HTTP requests including a redundant current-month comparison; a historical sweep alone would currently have a baseline of **85 requests** (80 reports + one form + the observed authentication overhead), before splits/retries. This is an estimate derived from measured calls, not a production traffic guarantee. Foreground traffic depends on the unioned date inventory; set/report a per-run192 collection-request budget and256 total transport budget initially, then measure under shadow before activation. Budget exhaustion, schema drift, totals mismatch or incomplete pages must checkpoint/retry or hold; never publish a complete inventory.

Use durable slot + selector + date-window identities, the existing queue, fenced leases, captured response times and least-recent-attempt fairness. The hourly dispatcher uses New York hour checks and deterministic local-date/slot identities for retries and DST. Each child has bounded collection/planning work, and recovery resumes missing children without replaying accepted effects. Expose per-lane last complete scope, oldest uncovered range, held rows, lag and failure state in Automations. Queue admission at a scheduled hour does not guarantee completion then; freshness targets assume healthy bounded backlog. This design is not yet installed.

## Shared access and live completion

Server authorization is **ordinary own assignment OR current eligible Pool entitlement**, applied before filtering/counting/sorting/cursors/detail/history. Implement Pool as a dedicated scope and DTO, not by removing assignment predicates globally. Rep eligibility uses the existing trusted active Desk mapping (`auto/on/off`, Granot username/reviewed provider mapping); an eligible reader without a reviewed callable identity still gets no invented call credit. Generic Admin/inactive/untrusted actors remain denied. Owner/Manager read authority alone is not caller identity.

The Pool filter counts the shared scope independently of receiver. Row/detail show assigned Rep or Unassigned, one shared due/completed/coverage-pending state, actual caller and completion time, restrictions, and optional advisory claim. Rep work is making real calls in the existing tools and receiving verified credit; shared access does not grant general Lead edits, foreign assignment changes, foreign money, or arbitrary historical contact data. Add exact BFF allowlists and strict mirrored DTOs. Cursor scope includes entitlement/filter versions.

Persist a committed shared invalidation hint after completion. Every currently authorized Pool reader must refetch the same duty; exit/revocation evicts cached rows/panels. Counts update with the rows. SSE is a hint, not correctness: reconnect/resnapshot and short foreground polling recover missed hints. A non-assigned Rep loses detail access on exit; the assigned Rep may retain ordinary access through its existing entitlement.

## Call qualification, replay and concurrency

Recommend the existing qualification rules: terminal provider-confirmed actual external outbound attempt, or reviewed answered inbound, uniquely associated to the Lead and reviewed eligible caller at **event time**. Outbound uses verified start time; inbound uses handling/answer time. Respect restrictions, contact hours, spacing and coverage watermarks. SMS, clicks, claim, internal/transfer legs, missed inbound, in-progress calls and ambiguous association do not complete the duty. Uncertain provider coverage is pending, not a false miss.

Normalize duplicate webhook/Call Log evidence and transfer legs to a canonical provider-account/call identity. The same canonical event cannot add another duty completion or another actual-call credit. Distinct real calls remain separately attributed even if simultaneous. Production must atomically insert/reuse the event and compare-and-set the unique daily duty, with transaction retry on conflicts. Stable attribution selects earliest qualifying event time then canonical event ID; late earlier evidence may correct the winner with audit/invalidation, without adding quota or stealing the distinct actual caller's event count. Claims are optional two-minute advisory coordination and cannot veto a qualified nonclaimant call. Corrections/revoked evidence recompute only the original duty date under audited event versions.

The local prototype models serialized event arrivals and stable event-ID replay. It does not emulate Mongo transactions, corrections, historical identity validity or network delivery. Before shipping, isolated replica proofs must cover simultaneous workers, same-event payload conflicts, retries after commit, late/out-of-order capture, provider correction, ambiguous numbers, closed periods and SSE/resnapshot access revocation. No client endpoint may mark a duty complete.

## Assigned workload, goals and calendar

Recommended workload display: **assigned Pool Leads due / assigned Pool Leads fulfilled today**, with shared completion counting toward the assigned Rep's workload even when a teammate calls. Team Pool completion is counted once. Actual outbound goal credit goes only to the real caller; the assigned Rep receives no fictional outbound call. Keep existing `all_outbound` and `eligible_new_quoted` scopes unchanged, show a separate Pool-call breakdown, and never rewrite frozen history. Under all_outbound, a qualified Pool outbound counts once; answered inbound can fulfill shared cadence with zero outbound goal credit. Under eligible_new_quoted, Pool does not silently enter the narrowed headline. This meets assigned workload participation without changing personal goal schedules or double-counting real calls.

Recommend the configured shared New York calendar: currently **Monday–Sunday [08:00,20:00)**, explicit Owner-closed dates waived. A Rep's off/zero-goal day does not pause or multiply the Lead's shared duty. Entry through **19:30 inclusive** opens one prospective call due at20:00; later entry starts the next working date. No Pool initial-response clock, automatic holidays, catch-up debt or prepayment. Same-day exit/reentry retains the same duty and earlier completion. Recommend allowing an already-qualified same-date ordinary call to fulfill the Pool slot when it complied with that date's gates; preserve its original attribution and add no new goal credit. The prototype's initial call gate starts at its recorded entry; cross-mode transfer is specified here for approval and remains an implementation requirement.
