# Granot and Reporting slimming release

November 18, 2026 · Vantage Movers · New York time unless marked UTC.

## Release scope

The Owner authorized immediate retirement of dashboard-initiated Granot automations and custom Reporting, including unfinished work. The scheduled Estimates collector, extension sync, official Master Sheets and CSI audit remain in service. The baseline was 7.91 GB of logical BSON in the receipt, observation and decision collections, 2.23 GB in automation runs and 430.6 MB in the Sales Intelligence queue.

The application now gives scheduled runs and settled evidence a checksum-verified recovery path in private Cloud Storage. The original 24-hour run execution deadline still applies. Technical receipt retries, dead-letter requeue, the separate unmatched-Lead retry clock and domain command idempotency continue independently of archive upload and restore. Routine reads use day counts, current Lead facts, business changes and case-owned evidence.

## Optimization handback

Eight application reads after historical deletion recovered job history and exact closed-operation replay from the archive. For the two verified cohorts together, 6,045,419 logical BSON bytes were removed after accounting for canary enrollment and the new replay/catalog records. Other enrolled run metadata and newly materialized facts are outside those two cohorts.

The two-pass enrollment examined 33,024 run IDs and enrolled 16,500 old scheduled runs. The second pass after the maximum old worker lifetime found zero late additions. Replaying the completed migration reproduced the saved checkpoint. All 23 remaining rows with dates in the old TTL index were independently examined, and no eligible scheduled run was unprotected. Four due runs then closed through the normal cron. At 20:27 UTC there were 14,314 due runs, the oldest from November 12 at 21:00 UTC. The preceding day produced 2,162 scheduled runs on a mix of cadences. Four reviews every two minutes allow at most 2,880/day, leaving 718/day before holds, failures and outages at that measured arrival rate. A full five-slot day must establish the realized rate.

The checkpoint, generation-pinned restore paths, per-collection bytes and post-delete reader results are available in the [aggregate archive verification](evidence/granot-slimming-archive-release-2026-11-18.json).

The provider's existing disk autoscaling started at 20:14:30 UTC and changed the M10 storage target to 17 GB. The team paused bulk writes at their saved checkpoints. The scheduled run collection had gained 398.7 MB of file allocation and 402.5 MB of reusable space between 19:27 and 20:16 UTC on the measured primary. That same member lost 1.10 GB of filesystem free space in the interval. Logical BSON deletion, reusable WiredTiger pages and filesystem free space are separate measures.
