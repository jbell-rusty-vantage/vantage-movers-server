# Sales Intelligence jobs and Sales Outreach production release

On November 19 the historical pass removed **233,735 full jobs** and reduced **177,836 more** to replay receipts, saving **212,054,586 logical MongoDB bytes (202.23 MiB)** at commit. No MongoDB lookup collection was added. The measured working set went from 593,303 rows / 441.14 MiB at 16:06:55 New York to 361,104 rows / 240.09 MiB at 16:39:13. New work continued throughout. The archive holds 38.72 MiB compressed across 282 packs and five range catalogs. Atlas retains its allocated space for reuse. ## What shipped

The capture producer and overlapping receipt-watermark scan validate dataset and canonical input hash on the existing unique key before returning its original job ID. A completed capture skips its queue wake. A runnable capture can receive another wake. Mailbox notifications sharing the same ten-second intent reuse the same verified identity. New revisions and mailbox buckets take the ordinary enqueue path. Active Outreach refresh nominations advance requested and active generations in a single MongoDB update. The worker still consumes only the generation it claimed. [Capture producer](../../src/services/numberActivity/webhookFanout.ts) · [Mailbox producer](../../src/services/ringcentral/repSms/intent.ts) · [Subject refresh generations](../../src/services/salesOutreach/evaluation/refreshRequests.ts)

For known routine Outreach results, the completion transaction validates the original producer key, references, dataset, payload hash and committed outcome. It converts the leased job in place under its original ID, unexpired lease, owner and epoch. The receipt retains its unique dedupe key, hash, dataset, subject and input identity, stage, creation and completion time, and three-day expiry. The Lead-change receipt also retains its result. The worker drops spent lease, retry and priority fields. Other routine results are served from their business projections. This costs one completion update. A conflicting replay still fails and an old queue delivery cannot reclaim the receipt. Ordinary completions outside the proven cohort keep the existing fourteen-day result and diagnostics. Pending, retry, paused, disputed and dead-letter work stays available for its own recovery.
