# Daylight capacity and the cost of an official Booking

**The released workload does not support a three-year commitment to the fixed 17 GB M10.** At 17:39 New York on November 18, the limiting member had 2.924 GB to the 90%-used planning line. Three years allows 2.67 MB/day of net physical growth. The 13-slot historical mix projects 90,909 new Granot receipt chains and 412.87 MB/day of gross logical BSON. Even if every configured review retired one distinct average-size chain, 46,080 reviews/day would leave 44,829 arrivals/day uncovered. These units describe different stages of the workload. The model converts logical growth to occupied data and index bytes and tests reuse separately. The 17:00 slot also produced **17 incomplete-evidence holds**. For ten evenly sampled holds the original cached effect carried `ref: null` while the immutable Decision omitted `ref`. The strict verifier distinguishes those shapes. A read-only counterfactual then tested **all 17** against the deployed verifier. It supplied a comparison-only copy of the Decision effect when the cached reference was explicitly null, the Decision omitted it, and every other effect field matched. It kept the original cached result for archive packing and replay.

All 17 passed the complete sealed-plan and operation checks under that comparison. All **4,074 replay results** round-tripped byte-identically to the stored responses. The original runs contained **9,842,136 B** of hot BSON. Those 17 remain holds in the production ledger, and the model credits zero removals or physical savings for them. The next implementation candidate is this narrow verifier change. ## Booking and Cancellation decision

**Gate A is measured for the requested outcomes. Defer a Booking or Cancellation writer change.** Existing exact retries produce no additional writes or Sheet jobs. The one observed fresh-key no-change patch in 30 days preserved its own command identity. Its production receipt occupied 1,121 B. Removing it would save about 37 logical bytes/day at the month's frequency and would change retry behavior. The seven-day workload cost about 39 kB/day for the entire modeled canonical command and Sheet delivery path. That total includes work the business needs. No repeatable, behavior-preserving writer saving was demonstrated.
