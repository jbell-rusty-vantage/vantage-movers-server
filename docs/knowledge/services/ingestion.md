# Best Relocation Ingestion

The final reconciliation completed November 3. It created 272 exception Forms and 43 exception Calls and verified all ten scoped Bookings. The Source Company retains its separate lifecycle. [Final reconciliation and scope](../../lead-reporting-retirement/FINAL-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-03.md)

The external tracker adapter, final-import runner, admin history endpoints, cron and queue consumer have been removed. Saved command executions can replay their original result. A new command from external sheet ingestion is refused before its business operation.

Canonical Leads, their admission and review stamps, source receipts, Entity Changes, Bookings and Cancellations carry the retained business evidence. Call billing and Sheet reporting read those saved facts directly. [Call Lead billing](../../call-lead-billing.md)
