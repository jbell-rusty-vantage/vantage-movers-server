# Custom Reporting

The November 2026 server slimming retires custom report definitions, previews, runs, deliveries, and Owner Google Drive authorization and Picker. The server abandons existing custom report work at cutover. Sheets already created in Google Drive stay with their owner.

Official Master Leads, Master Booked and source-company projections use [Sheet Sync](./sheet-sync.md). Business metrics use [Insights](./insights.md).
