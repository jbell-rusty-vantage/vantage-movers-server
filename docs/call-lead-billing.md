# Call Lead billing and Master Leads

Ordinary Call Lead creation requires a finished inbound RingCentral call to a mapped queue. The owner-approved final Best Relocation reconciliation is a separate, bounded source-admission exception; it does not change RingCentral qualification or reopen public, scheduled external, or Granot Call creation. The Call Log vetter uses elapsed duration (at least 120 seconds); a rounded 120-second result without sufficient precision does not qualify a new call. The webhook and cron both use final Call Log vetting.

RingCentral eligibility reads use the saved queue qualification: a recognized Call Log qualification reason, an accepted transport, a Call Log or telephony session identity, and finite duration of at least 120 seconds. The historical answered-call reason is supported, including previously accepted records stored at exactly 120 seconds. Ingestion origin, a Granot job, matching contact details, an old sheet row, and booking status cannot establish qualification. Historical RingCentral records do not need modern route IDs. New RingCentral inserts also require the resolved route and assignment.

`leads/callLeadAdmission.ts` supplies `hasReportingEligibleCall` and `reportingEligibleCallLeadFilter`: saved RingCentral qualification, the closed native Best Relocation historical inventory, OR a valid `external_call_admission` for the final Best Relocation import. The protected stamp pins policy `best-relocation-final-import-v1`, the approved tracker/Calls sheet, canonical Best Relocation company and Call feed, stable source row and content hash, real arrival, run/receipt IDs and native applied date. Its company/feed IDs must match the Lead. Public writes cannot set or change it; only the fenced, approved final-import command may attach it. It never fabricates `ringcentral.*` qualification. Form Fill, Form sibling matching, enrichment and prior-call duplicate candidates accept this same reporting admission. Official Booking/Cancellation rows remain reportable independently of Call eligibility. Granot may synchronize an existing Call Lead; ordinary owner/API flows may create Forms or attach existing Leads to Bookings.

Historical preservation is a read policy, not ingestion. `legacyBestRelocationCalls.ts` pins exactly 69 opaque canonical IDs captured on November 3 at 21:43:54.101Z, manifest version `best-relocation-legacy-reporting-v1`, sorted-ID SHA-256 `8ddf4d0e2e120bad4562a4cc93dd4961557bd8c53153dcf973b21a8d3648d990`. Each ID must still match the canonical Best Relocation company, Call feed and source slug. Unknown IDs and foreign or malformed scopes do not qualify through this rule. It adds no stamp, RingCentral fact, revision, source receipt, checker classification or Lead. Existing duplicate/Form Fill/unmatched/No-Sync decisions stay intact. The full native inventory remains restricted operator evidence.

## Reporting disposition

Reporting-eligible, matched Calls are duplicates for reporting when canonical `duplicate`, `form_fill`, or a trusted imported duplicate review is set. Trusted bad reviews and confirmed same-call final parent `Missed` results arriving from 01:00 inclusive to 07:00 exclusive in America/New_York are bad. Duplicate wins while retaining all reasons. Neither exception is billable. Ordinary No-Sync affects delivery only. Unknown or conflicting final results do not become missed calls by inference.

## Form Fill convergence and final evidence ordering

Form Fill compares exact normalized caller phone within the canonical Source Company across its form/call granularities. A canonical company disagreement cannot be overridden by a legacy slug; slug fallback is allowed only when the candidate has no canonical company. Eligible Forms are non-duplicate, with no age window. Repeated Form/Form and Call/Call rules retain their exact-granularity/time contracts.

Form creation marks at most 25 matching Calls immediately. Both arrival paths and relevant canonical Lead corrections/adoptions persist `form_fill_work` in the owning Mongo transaction. The existing authenticated queued Sheet Sync cron drains at most 20 pages of 25 candidate Calls before Sheets delivery, reloading current Form/Call evidence. A full page commits its cursor with the changed Calls, revisions, EntityChanges and queued Sheet Sync intents. Exact phone verification follows the regex sieve; read-only Form detection scans 100-row pages until a valid match or exhaustion. Interrupted ticks resume; concurrent drains/writes retry under Mongo transaction conflict rules. Failed work remains durable, delays 60 seconds and does not block other due tasks or Sheets. Its count/deferred count is returned by the cron, and failures log `lead.form_fill.work_deferred`.

This is eventual convergence: a concurrently arriving Form/Call can briefly appear billable until the follow-up tick. Production recovery requires queued Sheet Sync and its existing five-minute cron. Deployment does not backfill historical Leads with no new event. Phone/source, duplicate and admission changes emitted through EntityChange schedule fresh work; direct compatibility helper calls that emit no EntityChange do not acquire a new correction guarantee. Form Fill remains a retained positive fact: deletion, phone/source movement or making a Form duplicate does not automatically clear prior saved classification. Clearing historic proof requires a separately reviewed policy and repair. A newly eligible Form or admitted Call is reevaluated; there is no blanket reset or global automatic backfill.

Newer identical final Call Log results advance the observation watermark through the existing canonical command, without a semantic revision, EntityChange or Sheet Sync job. Older/equal observations are ignored. A later semantic correction retains the normal atomic revision/audit/outbox path. This closes the 08:05 Missed, 08:07 identical, delayed 08:06 Accepted ordering gap; arrival time still controls overnight classification.

Duplicate Calls and Bad Call Leads cost zero in effective reporting. They remain stored Leads. Form Fill alone never merges/closes a Desk subject; a verified Granot Form sibling and official Booking/Cancellation retain their separate operational authority. Overnight reporting-bad does not set `bad_lead`. [Review, verification and release limits](call-billing-review-2026-11-04.md).
