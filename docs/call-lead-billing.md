# Call Lead billing and Master Leads

Ordinary Call Lead creation requires a finished inbound RingCentral call to a mapped queue. The owner-approved final Best Relocation reconciliation is a separate, bounded source-admission exception; it does not change RingCentral qualification or reopen public, scheduled external, or Granot Call creation. The Call Log vetter uses elapsed duration (at least 120 seconds); a rounded 120-second result without sufficient precision does not qualify a new call. The webhook and cron both use final Call Log vetting.

RingCentral eligibility reads use the saved queue qualification: a recognized Call Log qualification reason, an accepted transport, a Call Log or telephony session identity, and finite duration of at least 120 seconds. The historical answered-call reason is supported, including previously accepted records stored at exactly 120 seconds. Ingestion origin, a Granot job, matching contact details, an old sheet row, and booking status cannot establish qualification. Historical RingCentral records do not need modern route IDs. New RingCentral inserts also require the resolved route and assignment.

`leads/callLeadAdmission.ts` supplies `hasReportingEligibleCall` and `reportingEligibleCallLeadFilter`: saved RingCentral qualification OR a valid `external_call_admission` for the final Best Relocation import. The protected stamp pins policy `best-relocation-final-import-v1`, the approved tracker/Calls sheet, canonical Best Relocation company and Call feed, stable source row and content hash, real arrival, run/receipt IDs and native applied date. Its company/feed IDs must match the Lead. Public writes cannot set or change it; only the fenced, approved final-import command may attach it. It never fabricates `ringcentral.*` qualification. Form Fill, Form sibling matching, enrichment and prior-call duplicate candidates accept this same reporting admission. Official Booking/Cancellation rows remain reportable independently of Call eligibility. Granot may synchronize an existing Call Lead; ordinary owner/API flows may create Forms or attach existing Leads to Bookings.

## Reporting disposition

`leads/callLeadReportingDisposition.ts` owns the reporting/billing decision. Reporting-eligible, matched Calls are duplicates for reporting when canonical `duplicate`, `form_fill`, or a trusted imported duplicate review is set. Trusted bad reviews and confirmed same-call final parent `Missed` results arriving from 01:00 inclusive to 07:00 exclusive in America/New_York are bad. Duplicate wins while retaining all reasons. Neither exception is billable. Ordinary No-Sync affects delivery only. Unknown or conflicting final results do not become missed calls by inference.

`external_sheet_review` is protected, versioned source evidence applied through `reconcileExternalSheetReviewV1`, with an Owner initiator, checksum, revision check, Entity Change, command replay and queued Sheet Sync in one lease-fenced transaction. Arbitrary annotations remain restricted data. Public create/update contracts cannot write the fact. For Forms, the owner-selected exclusive Bad Form Leads destination preserves canonical duplicate, Booking and Cancellation facts; stale ordinary Form memberships are removed only after the destination is confirmed. Public mark-bad restrictions remain.

Insights loads all facts the decision uses and retains separate duplicate/bad counters. Compatibility lead-cost queries apply the same Mongo predicate before aggregation. Custom Reporting keeps its versioned columns and uses the effective quality/billing decision in their values. Official Bookings and Cancellations remain reportable. Existing exported invoices are not rewritten.
