## Decisions made unattended

The packet leaves these mechanics to engineering; each choice is the fail-safe one (no fabricated miss, no guessed credit).

1. **Cadence encodings** (CONTRACTS: "exact encoding engineering-owned"): defined in `engine/policy.ts` with the CONTRACTS field names; FINAL-01 values in `approvedStartingValues.ts`. The P05d priority map is not a CONTRACTS `cadence` field, so it is not in this encoding (S1 owns the mapping, §6.3).
2. **Spacing anchor** moves only when a call actually earns cadence credit (P02e "between cadence-credited call starts"). A spaced call with nothing open does not move it.
3. **Callbacks are not spacing-gated**: any qualifying attempt in the 15-minute window fulfills the callback (P06e "regardless of answer"); that call then becomes the spacing anchor. Routine credit from the same call still needs spacing.
4. **Callback cancelled/replaced before its appointment**: the suspension ends at that instant; requirements already open at that moment stay suspended (no sudden debt); later windows resume.
5. A callback also suspends the unfinished initial response and hides an overdue initial-response prompt until the appointment (P06e "routine Call requirements and Call catch-up prompts").
6. **Initial response** stays its own outstanding item until a qualifying call (any hour, P07g) satisfies it; it is not folded into catch-up. On the arrival date its deadline is the first call slot's deadline; one call satisfies both.
7. **Catch-up** groups per policy period and channel; a routine window joins it when it stops being creditable unmet (closing, or a termination after its deadline). The period's end supersedes it (never revived, P05f).
8. **Pending vs missed**: a deadline after the channel's `complete_through`, or unconfirmed evidence in the window, makes the item `pending`; `complete_through: null` (e.g. SMS not connected) makes the verified count `null`.
9. **Restrictions**: a window open at the restriction start whose deadline already passed stays a genuine miss; windows opening during `[start, resume)` are waived; a callback whose appointment or window falls inside a restriction becomes `blocked_reschedule`.
10. **Reentry/activation prior credit**: calls on the start date before the start, made while a *different* New/Quoted period was active (activation: any verified call that date), spaced among themselves. An earlier same-date SMS pre-fulfills the start-date SMS (reuse, no new credit).
11. A transition before opening keeps the cap of two, all calls due at closing (P05f "no fabricated noon miss").
12. **P06b**: "unsuccessful" = contact-event `outcome: "unanswered"` only; window `(as_of − 24 h, as_of]`.
13. `last_interaction_at` includes confirmed missed inbound calls (SPECIFICATION §7 "external settled call") and inbound SMS.
14. The channel `required` count for a date includes a callback due that date.
15. During the run I once ran a whole-repo `tsc --noEmit` outside `heavy.sh` (~1 min) before switching to a scratch tsconfig scoped to `src/services/salesOutreach/**`. No other heavy command ran outside the lock.
