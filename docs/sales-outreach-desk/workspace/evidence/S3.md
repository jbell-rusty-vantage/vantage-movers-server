## Decisions made unattended

1. **Reconcile keeps its own sync step.** The packet says "move ISync out of the 5-minute reconcile". To keep the window reconcile and nightly sweep literally unchanged, the reconcile still runs its sync step on its own minutes (and outside staffed hours); the minute lane runs on the other four minutes of every five in staffed hours and yields on the reconcile's minute (UTC minute ≡ 3 mod 5). Net effect is the same: one ISync per staffed minute, one token chain, one lease. The reconcile now waits up to 20 s for a lease the minute lane holds instead of skipping its 5-minute window.
2. The lane runs only when `SALES_INTELLIGENCE_CALL_LOG_SYNC=on` (not `shadow`: shadow only counts and the window stays authoritative) and under `SALES_INTELLIGENCE_CAPTURE_CALL_LOG`. No new env flag.
3. The lane never bootstraps a token (FSync belongs to the reconcile, which owns `known_complete_through`); it never moves `known_complete_through`. It records `isync_lane.last_success_at` for freshness instead.
4. The staffed window [07:45, 20:30) New York for the ISync lane and the SMS poll is a capture-operations constant from RINGCENTRAL-CAPTURE §4/§7, not desk policy, so it is code, not configuration.
5. **Refresh narrowing** applies only with Call Log Sync `on` (due +5 min, retries 5/15 min, skip any row already in the Call Log); off/shadow keep the old 90 s schedule so nothing regresses where ISync is not running.
6. **Wake seam is inert** (`OUTREACH_CONTACT_CHANGE_CONSUMER_READY = false`) until SRV-6 registers the consumer, so no job without a consumer can pile up; even then it enqueues only when the desk configuration wants contact evidence.
7. **Light lane budget** (40 high / 10 low) is code constants, not env (no new env names); Heavy env knobs unchanged.
8. **Verification failure answers 403** and stores nothing. Deliveries for subscriptions with no stored token (today's `calls` subscription until the operator adds one), foreign subscriptions and handshakes are accepted as before; a token lookup failure also accepts (the receipt store would fail anyway).
9. The daily cron never creates or recreates `rep_sms` and never adds a token to `calls`; both are operator actions (`ops/ringcentral/outreach-subscriptions.ts --apply`), per §6 "creating the subscription is a separate, user-authorized step". The cron does renew and `PUT` the owned `rep_sms` subscription on filter drift or a missing token (owned-only, idempotent).
10. **Verification token** = 16 random bytes as 32 hex characters, stored in `ringcentral_webhook_subscriptions.verificationToken` (needed to verify deliveries); never logged or printed.
11. **P07e sender check**: when the reviewed link recorded the rep's numbers (`rc_direct_numbers`, `rc_sms_sender_number`) the outbound sender must be one of them, else `pending_identity` (`shared_sender`); when the link recorded none, the owning reviewed mailbox stands (nothing to compare against).
12. **SMS FSync history**: one FSync of up to 250 most recent SMS from 7 days back per mailbox; when RingCentral reports `olderRecordsExist`, `coverage_from` starts at the oldest record received rather than paging the message list (today's credit and Last interaction are covered; older history is reported as outside coverage, never as a zero).
13. `canonical_logical_id` = `<account>:<message id>`; one row per mailbox copy. Cross-mailbox dedupe of one logical message is left to SRV-6 (CONTRACTS: shared senders stay ambiguous until proven).
14. A mailbox sync answered 403 completes the job `permission_denied` (no retry storm); the 5-minute poll keeps trying. The E01 proof is the operator's step before turning capture on.
15. Rep SMS crons/drain do not need a CSI env flag beyond the existing `CAPTURE_WEBHOOK` for the recovery drain; the poll route always runs and gates on the desk control.
16. Package.json scripts were not added (S1 edits the same block); the commands are documented here and in the Service doc.
