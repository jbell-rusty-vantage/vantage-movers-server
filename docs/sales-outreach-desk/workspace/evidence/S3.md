## Decisions made unattended

1. **Reconcile keeps its own sync step.** The packet says "move ISync out of the 5-minute reconcile". To keep the window reconcile and nightly sweep literally unchanged, the reconcile still runs its sync step on its own minutes (and outside staffed hours); the minute lane runs on the other four minutes of every five in staffed hours and yields on the reconcile's minute (UTC minute ≡ 3 mod 5). Net effect is the same: one ISync per staffed minute, one token chain, one lease. The reconcile now waits up to 20 s for a lease the minute lane holds instead of skipping its 5-minute window.
2. The lane runs only when `SALES_INTELLIGENCE_CALL_LOG_SYNC=on` (not `shadow`: shadow only counts and the window stays authoritative) and under `SALES_INTELLIGENCE_CAPTURE_CALL_LOG`. No new env flag.
3. The lane never bootstraps a token (FSync belongs to the reconcile, which owns `known_complete_through`); it never moves `known_complete_through`. It records `isync_lane.last_success_at` for freshness instead.
4. The staffed window [07:45, 20:30) New York for the ISync lane and the SMS poll is a capture-operations constant from RINGCENTRAL-CAPTURE §4/§7, not desk policy, so it is code, not configuration.
5. **Refresh narrowing** applies only with Call Log Sync `on` (due +5 min, retries 5/15 min, skip any row already in the Call Log); off/shadow keep the old 90 s schedule so nothing regresses where ISync is not running.
6. **Wake seam is inert** (`OUTREACH_CONTACT_CHANGE_CONSUMER_READY = false`) until SRV-6 registers the consumer, so no job without a consumer can pile up; even then it enqueues only when the desk configuration wants contact evidence.
7. **Light lane budget** (40 high / 10 low) is code constants, not env (no new env names); Heavy env knobs unchanged.
8. **Verification failure answers 403** and stores nothing. Deliveries for subscriptions with no stored token (today's `calls` subscription until the operator adds one), foreign subscriptions and handshakes are accepted as before; a token lookup failure also accepts (the receipt store would fail anyway).
9. The daily cron never creates or recreates `rep_sms` and never adds a token to `calls`; both are operator actions (`ops/ringcentral/outreach-subscriptions.ts --apply`), per §6 "creating the subscription is a separate, user-authorized step". The cron does renew and `PUT` the owned `rep_sms` subscription on filter drift or a missing token (owned-only, idempotent).
10. **Verification token** = 16 random bytes as 32 hex characters, stored in `ringcentral_webhook_subscriptions.verificationToken` (needed to verify deliveries); never logged or printed.
11. **P07e sender check**: when the reviewed link recorded the rep's numbers (`rc_direct_numbers`, `rc_sms_sender_number`) the outbound sender must be one of them, else `pending_identity` (`shared_sender`); when the link recorded none, the owning reviewed mailbox stands (nothing to compare against).
12. **SMS FSync history**: one FSync of up to 250 most recent SMS from 7 days back per mailbox; when RingCentral reports `olderRecordsExist`, `coverage_from` starts at the oldest record received rather than paging the message list (today's credit and Last interaction are covered; older history is reported as outside coverage, never as a zero).
13. `canonical_logical_id` = `<account>:<message id>`; one row per mailbox copy. Cross-mailbox dedupe of one logical message is left to SRV-6 (CONTRACTS: shared senders stay ambiguous until proven).
14. A mailbox sync answered 403 completes the job `permission_denied` (no retry storm); the 5-minute poll keeps trying. The E01 proof is the operator's step before turning capture on.
15. Rep SMS crons/drain do not need a CSI env flag beyond the existing `CAPTURE_WEBHOOK` for the recovery drain; the poll route always runs and gates on the desk control.
16. Package.json scripts were not added (S1 edits the same block); the commands are documented here and in the Service doc.

### Decisions made unattended (phase B)

1. **Subject-less contact events.** A source without a unique active subject keeps one row with `subject_id: null`, so M1 all-outbound and "Other outbound" are recountable from `sales_outreach_contact_events` alone.
   - `subject_id` became nullable.
   - Fields added: `association`, `subject_workflow`, `outcome`, `goal_credit`, `goal_scope_eligible`, `originating_inbound`, `input_fingerprint`.
   - The model is otherwise unchanged; indexes are unchanged.
2. **One row per source.** The `_id` is derived from `(source_kind, source_id)`, so concurrent writers collide; one row per source is enough because IMPL-07 associates at most one subject.
3. **`goal_agent_id` is set for awaiting attempts too.** This lets the rep-day count "awaiting confirmation". S2's `goalCreditAgent` still credits only `verification: confirmed`.
4. **Count scope.** The day after the earliest *completed* enrollment `apply`'s `activation_at` (New York date) uses `eligible_new_quoted`. The activation date and earlier days use `all_outbound`. This comes from stored enrollment state, never env.
   - `unattributed` = confirmed attempts without an eligible New/Quoted subject, in both scopes. That includes ambiguous association and subjects in discretion/none/review.
   - The p07b `ambiguous_lead` "no goal credit" applies to the M2 scope. M1 counts the call (FAST-TRACK M1).
5. **Projection watermark (S1's open question): decided yes.**
   - The calls sweep stores `sales_intelligence_sync_state` scope `outreach_contact_calls`:
     - `known_complete_through` = the capture `known_complete_through` read at the start of a pass that caught up;
     - `cursor.outreach_coverage_from` = the bootstrap start.
   - Row coverage = `callsCoverageForDay(min(capture − 2 min, watermark))`; a day before `coverage_from` is `unknown`.
   - **S1's read should also apply this watermark to reps *without* a row.** That is S1 code; see the handoff.
   - The watermark can be optimistic by up to the 2-minute overlap for a capture write that commits late; the overlap re-scan repairs it.
6. **Sweep bootstrap** = New York midnight of `today − transition.backfill_lookback_days` (90 in FINAL-01; 0 when unset). Older calls are outside the derived evidence, and their days read `unknown`, never zero.
7. **Rep-day fingerprint** keeps the coverage *state*, not the moving instant, so rows are not rewritten every minute. A row's stored `known_complete_through` can lag; S1's merge uses capture coverage whenever the row is not worse.
8. **Goal snapshot.** Today's row has `configuration_version: null`, so the read resolves from the active configuration. The first recount after the day ends freezes it, and the refresh pass guarantees that recount within a minute. A frozen snapshot is never re-frozen. Goals that cannot be resolved (no default goal) leave `goal: null` unfrozen, and the read fails closed.
9. **Rows only for activity.** No row is written for a rep-day whose recount has no confirmed, awaiting or unattributed attempt and no prior row; restricted-only activity writes nothing.
10. **Not actual attempts.** The no-attempt Call Log results are `Internal Error`, `Restricted`, `Blocked`, `International Disabled`/`International Restriction`, `Suspended account`/`Account Suspended` and `Stopped`. Every other terminal Call Log outbound counts as an actual attempt, including `Call Failure` (P07a `verified_failed_connection`). The provider mapping is unproven (`provider_status_mapping_proven: false`).
11. **Monitoring.** The call-level `monitoring` flag does not exclude a call. Only monitoring legs and roles are ignored when picking the initiator or handler.
12. **Answered-inbound handler** = the first connected user extension, by party `answered_at` or connected leg start. A tie is ambiguous; no handler = missed.
13. **Restrictions.** The interval is `[createdAt, min(until, resolved_at))` for all origins. The inert AI-origin rows stay blocking (FAST-TRACK). Restricted contact keeps its history row with zero credit.
14. **Association uses current attachments.** Attachment state is not effective-dated, so a later attachment change re-derives when the call row is touched or swept again.
    - **Open item:** an attachment created after the call has no wake into SRV-6. M1 counts are unaffected. M2 subject credit for such calls waits until the call row changes, or until an attachment-driven re-derivation is added (see open questions).
15. **SMS duplicate copies.** Only the lowest-`_id` mailbox copy of a `canonical_logical_id` credits. A new index `sod_rsms_logical` supports this.
16. **`outreach_evaluate` identity** from contact changes is `sod:evaluate:<subject>:r<rev>:contacts:<digest of event versions>`. `subject_key` and `input_refs` match S1's, and `input_revision` is the subject revision.
17. **Cron.** S3's own route `/api/cron/sales-outreach-contact-events` runs in a separate router file, to avoid conflicts with S1's cron router. The budget is ≈ 50 s per minute.
18. **P07g originating inbound.** `originating_inbound` is true for a confirmed reviewed-rep answered inbound whose uniquely associated subject is a Call Lead with `ringcentral.telephony_session_id` equal to the call's `telephony_session_id`.
19. **`outreach_goal` live hint.** S1 phase 4b had not merged when this phase finished. `contacts/goalPublish.ts` `publishOutreachGoalChanges` is a no-op TODO seam, called after every committed rep-day write.
