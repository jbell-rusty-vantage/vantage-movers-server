# CSI-03 independent review and resolution

Reviewer: separate Opus 5 code-reviewer agent, read-only over the CSI-03 patch (new `numberActivity` fan-out/recovery/worker/dispatch modules, `ringcentral/webhook-subscription-lifecycle.ts`, cron router, queue consumer, ops script, replica proof) and the additive edits to CSI-01 and qualification-owned files, allowed to run the isolated suites and its own probes against the local replica. First pass: **Request Changes** (one must-fix, seven should-fix, four nits). Second pass after resolution: **Approve**, with one deployment condition and three non-blocking residuals, all three of which were then closed. Findings below were confirmed by the reviewer by execution where stated; resolutions were implemented by Fable (main implementer) and re-proven on the isolated replica ([CHECKS.md](CHECKS.md)).

The reviewer explicitly verified as correct and not to be re-litigated: receipt-before-job-before-ack ordering in its own transaction, flag independence from `RINGCENTRAL_WEBHOOK_ENABLED` in both directions, uuid dedupe with receipt-id fallback (one job per duplicate uuid), `input_refs` as the only receipt source and strict `{ job_id }` payloads, the `capture_projection` stage fence leaving foreign stages pending with `attempts` untouched, lease fencing (expired holder can neither complete nor fail after a successor claims; completion after projection commit is safe under CSI-02 semantic replay and dedupe-keyed downstream jobs), per-session retry classification, the fenced state write and single lease holder for recovery, the byte-for-byte inbound builders, foreign subscriptions never renewed/deleted/replaced, cron auth and bounded 500s, queue trigger registration mirroring the Granot consumer, the `numberActivity` import boundary, and that the CSI-01 shared edits are additive (CSI-01 15/15, CSI-02 12/12 unchanged). Nothing in the patch or its tests claims live RingCentral behavior.

## Second-pass residuals (non-blocking; all closed)

| # | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Scan sort `{receivedAt:1,_id:1}` had no serving index → SORT stage per page over the window. | Added `{provider:1, receivedAt:1, _id:1}` partial index (`telephonySessionId` string) to the receipt store's own index set (`webhook-capture.ts`, additive); `webhookReceiptsCollection()` calls the exported `ensureRingCentralWebhookEventIndexes()` so the cron process has it too. |
| R2 | Timestamp-only page cursor with inclusive `$gte`: a same-millisecond cluster larger than one page parked the scan (`budget_exhausted` forever). | Keyset paging on `(receivedAt, _id)` (`ReceiptCursor`, strict `$gt` after the last processed position). Replica test: 10 receipts sharing one millisecond, batch 3 → 4 pages, each ensured exactly once, `budget_exhausted: false`. |
| R3 | The scan had no wall-clock budget while the drain did. | `RecoveryConfig.deadlineMs` (`SALES_INTELLIGENCE_WEBHOOK_RECOVERY_DEADLINE_MS`, default 30 s) checked between pages → `budget_exhausted`. |

Alias/identity semantics (CSI-02), job dedupe keys, transaction boundaries of `enqueueCsiJob`/`completeCsiJob`, the decision to complete the job after per-session projection commits (accepted as safe), the inbound-only builders, and the decision not to gate capture recovery on the master read switch.
