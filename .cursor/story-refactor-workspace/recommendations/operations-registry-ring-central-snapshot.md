## Refresh and invalidation race

A shared `refreshPromise` coalesces concurrent reloads. `cacheGeneration` changes on invalidation; if a load finishes for an older generation, refresh loops before publishing it. Successful refresh records resolver telemetry, and failed refresh may serve a cached snapshot only within the last-known-valid age bound. Effective assignment intervals are `[effective_from, effective_until)` at call start. Diagnostic all-number lists include historical intervals; active-number lists resolve at the requested instant. Preserve that distinction and do not replace call-start resolution with process-now matching.
